Personal Statement

My Journey as a Tutor

Welcome to my personal statement. This page introduces who I am, what guides my approach to tutoring, and the goals I hope to achieve through supporting students.

GET TO KNOW ME

Who I Am

I am Azola Mlotywa, and I see tutoring as an opportunity to contribute to students' academic development. I value patience, respect, communication and a willingness to learn. I believe that a supportive learning environment can help students become more confident in their abilities.

01

My Tutoring Philosophy

I believe that learning should be interactive, inclusive and focused on understanding. As a tutor, I aim to explain concepts clearly, encourage questions and give students opportunities to practise what they have learnt.

02

My Goals

My goal is to help students improve their understanding, develop problem-solving skills and become more independent learners. I also want to continue improving my communication and tutoring skills.

03

My Values

I value patience, responsibility, respect, honesty and teamwork. I aim to treat students fairly, listen to their questions and create an environment where they feel comfortable participating.

04

My Commitment

I am committed to preparing for tutorials, reflecting on my experiences and learning from feedback. I understand that becoming an effective tutor requires continuous development and a willingness to improve.

MY PERSONAL MOTIVATION

Helping students discover what they are capable of.

I want my tutoring journey to be one of continuous learning, meaningful support and positive academic development.
