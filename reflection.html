MY LEARNING JOURNEY

Reflections

Reflecting on my learning experiences helps me understand my strengths, recognise areas for improvement and develop my skills as a tutor.

My Reflection on Tutoring

Being a tutor requires patience, preparation and a willingness to support students with different learning needs. Through learning about tutoring, I have come to understand that a tutor should not only explain information but also encourage students to participate, ask questions and become more confident.

I have also learned that effective communication is important when helping students understand difficult concepts. A tutor should create a welcoming learning environment where students feel comfortable asking for assistance.

What I Have Learned

01

Communication

I have learned the importance of explaining information clearly and listening carefully to students.

02

Preparation

Preparing tutorial activities and learning materials can help make learning more organised and effective.

03

Patience

Students learn at different speeds, so a tutor should be patient and willing to explain concepts in different ways.

Challenges and Areas for Improvement

One area I would like to improve is my ability to explain challenging topics in simple and understandable ways. I also want to improve how I manage tutorial time and encourage students who may be shy or unsure of their answers.

To improve, I will continue preparing before tutorials, learning from feedback and exploring different teaching strategies. I will also use appropriate digital tools to support learning and make educational resources easier to access.

My Future Goals as a Tutor

My goal is to become a more confident, supportive and effective tutor. I want to create a positive learning environment, help students understand their work and continue developing my communication, problem-solving and teaching skills.
