MY TUTORING PORTFOLIO

Training & Development

My facilitation training focuses on the Hands, Heart and Head approach. These three areas help me develop practical skills, positive relationships and knowledge to support students during tutoring.

The Hands, Heart and Head Approach

The Hands, Heart and Head approach encourages a balanced way of facilitating learning through practical action, emotional connection and understanding.

Hands – Practical Skills

Hands represent learning by doing. As a tutor, I can use activities, demonstrations and practical exercises to help students apply what they learn.

In tutoring: I guide students through examples and allow them to practise solving problems themselves.

Heart – Relationships and Care

Heart represents empathy, respect, patience and understanding. It reminds me to create a supportive environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and making mistakes.

In tutoring: I listen to students, encourage participation and treat everyone with respect.

Head – Knowledge and Understanding

Head represents thinking, knowledge, understanding and problem-solving. A facilitator needs to understand the subject and explain ideas clearly.

In tutoring: I explain concepts step by step, ask questions and help students develop independent thinking.

How I Apply These Skills as a Tutor

  • Hands: Use practical activities and guided exercises.
  • Heart: Build trust and encourage students to participate.
  • Head: Explain concepts and develop critical thinking.

Combining Hands, Heart and Head helps me aim for tutoring sessions that are practical, supportive and focused on understanding.
