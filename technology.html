Technology in Tutoring

Technology plays an important role in teaching and learning. Digital platforms help students access learning materials, communicate with tutors and participate in educational activities. I recognise the value of using technology to support learning and improve communication.

How Technology Supports Learning

Digital tools can help organise academic information, share resources and support communication between tutors and students. Different platforms provide different features to support the learning process.

Online Learning

Online learning platforms can provide access to academic resources, learning activities and important course information.

Communication

Digital communication tools help tutors and students exchange information, ask questions and receive announcements.

Collaboration

Online collaboration tools can support group discussions, virtual meetings and the sharing of learning materials.

Responsible Use of Technology

Technology should be used responsibly to support learning. Personal information must be protected, online information should be checked for reliability, and students' privacy must be respected. Tutors should also consider students who have limited access to devices or internet connectivity.
