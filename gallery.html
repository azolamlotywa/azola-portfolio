Student Feedback and Evaluation

Student feedback provides useful information about the tutoring experience. The following results are from the DCN II Tutor Feedback questionnaire, which received 13 responses.

Overall Tutoring Experience

Of the 13 responses, 61.5% rated the overall tutoring experience as excellent, 30.8% rated it as good and 7.7% rated it as average.

Reflection: Most respondents gave a positive rating. This feedback can help identify practices worth maintaining in future tutoring sessions.

Clarity of Tutor Explanations

Seven out of 13 respondents, or 53.8%, rated the clarity of the tutor's explanations as 5 out of 5.

Reflection: Clear explanations are important in tutoring. I can use student feedback to reflect on communication and find ways to explain difficult concepts more effectively.

What I Have Learned from This Evidence

The classroom photograph demonstrates the use of a technology-supported learning environment, while the feedback results provide information about students' experiences. Together, these examples highlight the importance of preparation, clear explanations and listening to student feedback.

Using Feedback to Improve

Maintain Strengths

Continue using clear explanations, relevant examples and appropriate learning resources.

Listen to Students

Consider student experiences and feedback when planning and delivering tutorials.

Keep Improving

Reflect on areas that need improvement and make adjustments to future tutoring sessions.
