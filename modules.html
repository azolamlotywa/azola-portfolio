MODULE OVERVIEW

Purpose of the Module

Data Communication and Networks II focuses on developing students' understanding of how devices communicate and how computer networks operate. It builds on networking knowledge and helps students develop the skills needed to understand, analyse and solve networking problems.

As a tutor, my role is to support students in understanding difficult concepts, practising relevant exercises and developing confidence in their learning.

LEARNING AREAS

Key Networking Concepts

Explore important networking concepts covered during tutorial sessions.

Network Fundamentals

Understand computer networks, network devices and how information moves between devices.

Protocols and Models

Learn how communication rules help devices exchange information.

IP Addressing

Explore how IP addresses identify devices on a network.

Network Troubleshooting

Learn to identify network problems and find possible solutions.

EXPECTED DEVELOPMENT

Learning Outcomes

Through tutorial support, I aim to help students strengthen their understanding of networking concepts, apply what they learn to exercises, explain their reasoning and approach networking problems with greater confidence.

These are general learning aims, not a replacement for the official learning outcomes published by Walter Sisulu University.

“Understanding grows when knowledge is shared, questions are welcomed and practice is encouraged.”
